Chapter 01

Introduction

Alpha Premier Attendance replaces the paper logbook at the front desk. Interns tap an RFID card, the screen shows their photo and greets them, and the time is saved.

What the system does

Attendance kiosk

Records one time in and one time out per intern per day with a card tap. It shows the photo, name and time, and speaks a greeting.

Bathroom key log

Tracks who has the male or female restroom key, when they took it and how long it has been out.

Live attendance

Shows today's time-ins and time-outs on the kiosk and, read-only, on any phone or PC on the office Wi-Fi.

Admin and payroll

Register cards, fix mistakes, add missed days, build semi-monthly intern payroll and print one payroll PDF per cutoff.

How it fits together

Solid arrow: always on. Dashed arrow: optional; scans never wait for it.

Five things to know before you start

  1. One tap in, one tap out. The first tap of the day is the time in. The second is the time out. After that the card answers "Attendance is already complete for today."
  2. The kiosk window must be in front. The card reader works like a keyboard. If another program is in front, the taps go there instead. You do not need to click any box first.
  3. No internet needed. Every tap is saved on the front-desk computer straight away. The Google Sheets copy catches up later by itself.
  4. Office hours are 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Lunch (12:00–1:00 PM) is unpaid. Tapping out at 6:00 PM or later flags the day for correction (Chapter 14).
  5. Closing the window does not stop it. The X button hides the app to the system tray. Scanning keeps working whenever the window is shown again.

System requirements

ItemRequirement
ComputerWindows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
Card reader125 kHz EM4100 USB reader working in keyboard mode (sends 10 digits + Enter). This is the default reader.
CardsOne 125 kHz card per intern; at least one extra card to use as an admin RFID card.
SpeakersOptional, for spoken greetings.
NetworkOptional. Office Wi-Fi for the live viewer; internet for updates, PIN-reset email and Google Sheets.
Installation rightsWindows administrator approval is needed once, when installing.

What's in this version

Version 0.2.14 is for interns only. Every person you register is paid as an intern at PHP 80.00 per day. The payroll documents are the Intern Payroll PDF and one payslip picture per intern (Generate Intern Payslips); older employee payslips and spreadsheet payroll exports are no longer part of the app.

NoteThe pay rules changed on 1 October 2026: the old weekly 15-minute grace period was removed. Days before that date keep the old rules, so past pay never changes. See Chapter 14.

Installing and starting the app

Do this once on the front-desk computer. It takes about 10 minutes.

Install

  1. Download the latest Alpha Premier Attendance_…_x64-setup.exe from the company's GitHub Releases page (ask IT for the link).
  2. Double-click the file. Approve the Windows administrator prompt.
  3. Follow the installer. It installs for all users and adds the WebView2 component if it is missing.
  4. Plug the USB card reader into the computer. No driver is needed.
  5. Open Alpha Premier Attendance from the Start menu. The kiosk screen appears (Chapter 3).

First-time setup checklist (administrator)

  1. Get admin access. A new installation has no PIN. Open Admin, click Forgot PIN? and set the first PIN with the emailed code (Chapter 7).
  2. Register an admin RFID card so you can unlock without the PIN (Chapter 8).
  3. Register every intern's card, with photo and gender (Chapter 8).
  4. Test: tap one intern card. You should see the photo and "Time In recorded successfully."
  5. Make the first backup (Chapter 12) and, if wanted, share the live viewer link (Chapter 6).

Recording attendance

For interns. It takes about one second.

To time in (arriving)

  1. Check the kiosk shows 1 Attendance and the green Ready pill.
  2. Hold your card flat on the reader until it beeps.
  3. Check the screen shows your photo and name and "Time In recorded successfully." You will also hear a greeting.
  4. Walk away. The screen returns to "ready" by itself after about 4 seconds.

To time out (leaving)

Tap the same card again at the end of the day. The screen shows "Time Out recorded successfully." and you hear "Goodbye, name".

Tap out before 6:00 PMA time-out at 6:00 PM or later is saved as LATE TIMEOUT. The kiosk still says "recorded successfully", but the voice warns that a manual correction is required and an administrator must fix the time (Chapter 9). The office does not pay overtime.
One time in and one time out per dayA third tap shows "Attendance is already complete for today." If you tapped out by mistake, ask an administrator to correct it.

Good habits

  • Forgot your card? Ask an administrator for an assisted time in (page "Manual entry and forgotten cards").

Manual entry (when the reader is not working)

  1. While the kiosk is ready, click Manual entry at the bottom.
  2. Type the card number printed on the card (usually 10 digits).
  3. Press Enter or click Record. The result appears just like a tap.
  4. Click Use card reader to go back to normal tapping. The reader is paused while manual entry is open.

Forgotten or broken card — assisted attendance

An administrator can record attendance for an intern who has no working card.

  1. Make sure the kiosk is on 1 Attendance. The administrator taps their admin RFID card.
  2. The Assisted Attendance window opens. It closes by itself after 25 seconds (see the "Auto-cancels in…" counter).
  3. Search for the intern by name, ID or department and click their name.
  4. Choose the Reason for assisted scan: Forgot RFID card, Defective RFID card or Other reason… (type the reason).
  5. Click Confirm attendance. The kiosk shows the normal result plus "Assisted by admin card".
Audit trailAssisted taps are marked Assisted in Attendance corrections with the admin card and reason, so they can be reviewed later.

Bathroom key log (kiosk)

There is one male and one female restroom key. The kiosk records who has each key, so the old handwritten sign-out sheet is no longer needed.

To take a key

  1. Press 2 or click 2 Bathroom Key Log at the top of the kiosk.
  2. Tap your card. The key that matches the gender on your profile is checked out to you ("Male floor key checked out").
  3. Return within 15 minutes, as the voice reminds you.

To return a key

  1. Put the key back and tap the same card again in Bathroom Key Log mode.
  2. The screen shows "floor key returned" and how many minutes it was out.
  3. Press 1 to switch the kiosk back to Attendance.

Sharing the list with the office (LAN viewer)

The front-desk computer can show today's list to managers on the same Wi-Fi. They only need a web browser; nothing is installed. The viewer is read-only: it never shows admin pages, payroll, photos or edit buttons.

  1. On the kiosk, open Live attendance. The viewer starts automatically. If it shows Stopped, click Start LAN viewer.
  2. When the panel shows Running, click Copy URL. The link looks like http://192.168.1.25:4173/attendance.
  3. Send the link to the manager. They open it on a phone or PC connected to the office Wi-Fi.
  4. Their page shows each time in and time out, newest first, with a status chip such as Live — streaming.

Other buttons: Open Local Viewer (test the page on this computer), Stop viewer, Refresh status.

If the office uses a viewer password

When IT has turned on password mode, add ?token= and the password to the end of the link, for example http://192.168.1.25:4173/attendance?token=YourPassword. Without it the page says "Viewer access required". The copied link does not include the password; add it yourself.

Chapter 07

Administrator access

The admin area is protected. Unlock it with the administrator PIN or a registered admin RFID card.

To unlock

  1. On the kiosk, click Admin at the bottom.
  2. Either tap your admin RFID card on the reader (it fills the box and unlocks by itself), or type the PIN and click Unlock admin.
  3. The admin screen opens on the Users and RFID tab.

Sessions and locking

  • An admin session lasts 15 minutes from unlocking (it is not extended by activity). Afterwards you see "Admin session expired. Please unlock again."
  • Click Lock (top-right) when you are done. Always lock before leaving the desk.
  • If you go back to the kiosk and return to Admin, you may need to unlock again.

PIN rules

  • 6 to 12 digits. Easy patterns such as 111111, 123456 or 654321 are refused.
  • A new installation has no PIN; use an admin card or set one with Forgot PIN?
  • The PIN belongs to this computer only; it is not shared with other installations. Changing it does not affect admin cards.
  • A wrong PIN or unknown card shows "The administrator PIN is invalid."

Forgot PIN — resetting by email

A one-time 6-digit code is emailed to the company inbox thealphapremiergroup@gmail.com. The computer needs internet for this.

The same Forgot PIN? link is in the kiosk's Admin setup window.

  1. On the unlock screen, click Forgot PIN?, then Email reset code.
  2. The screen shows a request ID. Open the company inbox and find the email whose subject has the same request ID.
  3. Type the Reset code, then the New PIN twice (Confirm new PIN).
  4. Click Set new PIN. You see "Admin PIN updated. Unlock with the new PIN." Unlock with the new PIN.

Already have a code from earlier? Click I already have a code. Need another? Click Send a new code — earlier codes then stop working.

Reset rules

  • A code works for 15 minutes and allows 5 wrong tries.
  • Wait 1 minute between requests. After 10 wrong codes in 24 hours, resets are blocked until the next day.
  • Admin RFID cards keep working throughout — the easiest way back in.

Users and RFID cards

Every intern needs a profile linked to their card. There are two ways to register: the quick Admin setup window on the kiosk (best for tapping new cards), or the Users and RFID tab.

Registering a card with Admin setup (recommended)

  1. On the kiosk, click Admin setup (or Setup this card after tapping an unknown card). The Associate RFID card window opens.
  2. 01 Unlock — tap your admin card or type the PIN, then click Unlock setup.
  3. 02 Scan card — tap the intern's card on the reader. The window shows New card (blank profile) or Existing card (current details).
  4. 03 Save user — under Register card as, keep Intern card and fill in User ID, Full name, Department / role, Status, Gender and an optional ID photo.
  5. Click Save user. You see "Card enrolled successfully." Click Scan another card for the next person, or Lock setup when finished.
Saving here never records attendanceRegistering or updating a card only changes the user list. To make an admin RFID card instead, choose Admin RFID card in step 4, give it a label (for example "Front desk admin card #1") and click Save admin card.

Close the window with X, Esc or by clicking outside it. If unlocking Admin elsewhere replaces this session, you see "Your setup session expired or was replaced" — unlock again.

Editing, deactivating and deleting users

Edit a user or replace a lost card

  1. Find the person with the search box ("Search users by name, ID, RFID, or department…").
  2. Click Edit on their row. Change the details — for a replacement card, type the new card number in RFID UID.
  3. Click Save user. Click Cancel edit to leave without saving.

Someone leaves

Set Status to Inactive and save. Their history and payroll stay; their card stops working.

Delete is permanentDelete removes the user and all of their attendance, bathroom logs and payroll. It cannot be undone. Prefer Inactive. Make a backup first if you must delete.

Several users at once

Tick the boxes on the left of the rows, then use Set Active (n), Set Inactive (n) or Delete selected (n). Clear selection unticks all.

Select only some usersType in the search box first, then click Select all: only the users shown by the search are selected. The count ("2 of 12 user(s) selected") confirms it before a bulk action.

Voice clips

Each intern's name can be spoken in the cloned "Ma'am Bea" voice. Click Regenerate to make a fresh clip (up to 2 minutes; "Queued…" or "Cloning…" is shown). Until it is ready the computer voice says the name.

Google Sheets DTR

Sync DTR (on a row) or Sync Interns to DTR (top of the list) copies interns to the "INTERN DTR" Google Sheet. Progress messages such as "Syncing intern 3 of 12" appear; it needs internet.

To correct a time (for example a late time-out)

  1. Pick the Date. Click the Late Time-out chip to see only the rows that need fixing.
  2. In the person's row, change Time out to the official time (for example 5:00 PM).
  3. Click Save on that row. You see "Saved" and the status changes to COMPLETED.
  4. If you still enter a time at or after 6:00 PM, you see "Saved — time-out is still after office hours, correction remains required."

Saving also re-prices that day's pay straight away. Times you do not touch keep their exact original seconds.

Deleting records

How each intern's pay is worked out

  • Only interns with at least one completed day in the period get a row.
  • Absent days = standard days − days worked. Each absence: −PHP 80.00.
  • Late and undertime: −PHP 10.00 per hour; half days: −PHP 40.00 (rules in Chapter 14).
  • Net Pay is never below zero for any single day. Open Calculation breakdown & payslip detail to see every deduction by date.

Columns such as HRA, SSS, PhilHealth, HDMF and Overtime are kept for the payroll sheet layout and are always PHP 0.00 for interns. Bank / A/C shows "CASH / 0000" when empty.

Reviewing and adjusting a draft

  1. Click Edit on the row. The window shows the attendance basic pay, late deduction and absent / half-day amounts.
  2. Enter a Manual Adjustment (PHP) — positive to add, negative to deduct — and the required Adjustment Reason.
  3. Check Total Earnings, Total Deductions and Net Pay, then click Save Changes.

Only the manual adjustment can be edited. To change days, late hours or half days, correct the attendance and generate again.

Finalizing

  1. When a row is correct, click Finalize and confirm "Finalize payroll?". Or tick rows and click Finalize selected (n).
  2. The row shows Finalized and can no longer be edited. Backdated attendance can no longer be added inside that period.
Regenerating keeps finalized payClicking Generate from attendance again for the same cutoff rebuilds only the draft rows; finalized rows are kept as they are (the yellow banner says "finalized records are kept"). Late time-outs are paid up to 5:00 PM, as the confirmation window says.
To redo a finalized rowDelete that row on purpose (its Delete button stays available, for example when an intern leaves), then generate again. Make a backup first.

Deleting payroll records

Click Delete on a row, or Delete all records for this cutoff (n) in the yellow banner, or tick rows and click Delete selected (n). Attendance is not affected; you can generate again afterwards.

Chapter 14

Attendance and pay rules

These rules apply to attendance from 1 October 2026. The app applies them automatically; this chapter explains the numbers you see.

RuleHow it works
Office hours8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Philippine time. A full day is 8 paid hours.
Lunch12:00–1:00 PM is unpaid and never counted as work.
Daily ratePHP 80.00 per day (PHP 10.00 per hour).
On timeTapping in up to 8:00:59 AM. Coming early earns nothing extra.
LateFrom 8:01:00 AM, every started hour late costs PHP 10.00, counted from 8:00. 8:08 = 1 hour; 9:00:01 = 2 hours. There is no grace period.
UndertimeEach hour (or part) short of the 8-hour day costs PHP 10.00 — but hours already charged as late are not charged twice.
Half dayArriving at 12:00 PM or later, or leaving by 1:00 PM, counts as a half day: PHP 40.00 is deducted as undertime, not late.
Late time-outTapping out at 6:00 PM or later marks the day LATE TIMEOUT for correction. Pay is worked out as if the time-out were 5:00 PM. No overtime.
MinimumA day's pay never goes below PHP 0.00.
AbsencesEach standard day not worked in the cutoff costs PHP 80.00.

Frequently asked questions

Does the kiosk need internet?

No. Taps, admin and payroll all work offline. Internet is only for updates, PIN-reset email, voice clip creation and Google Sheets.

What happens if the power goes out?

Every tap is saved the moment it is shown on screen. When the PC comes back, the app starts by itself (if automatic start is on).

Can two computers record attendance?

No. The front-desk computer is the only one that records. Other devices can only view through the live viewer.

Can an intern see their own pay?

Not in the app. Administrators print the Intern Payroll PDF and share it as the office decides.

Why does a time show "Late (57m)" but only 1 hour deducted?

The Arrival column shows minutes late; pay deducts per started hour (Chapter 14).

We changed the pay rules. Will past pay change?

No. Each day remembers the rules that priced it. New rules need a software update from IT.

Where are my files?

See Appendix B. In Admin, Show in Folder and Open backups folder take you straight there.
